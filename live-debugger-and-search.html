Live debugger and search

Live debugger. Keep a dialogue open while you Play In Editor.

  • The node each NPC is on glows green, and nodes already passed stay outlined.
  • With Follow play on, the graph keeps the playing node in view.
  • It works for barks too.

Find (Ctrl+F). Searches line text, speakers, options, conditions, pills and comments. Enter jumps to the next match, and matches are outlined.
